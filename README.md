## Overview
FashionBD is a modern e-commerce front-end interface designed for online fashion retailers[cite: 2]. It features a clean, user-friendly layout that highlights premium fashion items at affordable prices, making it easy for customers to browse and shop[cite: 2].

## Key Features
* **Intuitive Navigation:** Includes a prominent search bar, shopping cart access, and clear navigation links for Home, Products, Product Detail, Signup, and Login[cite: 2].
* **Hero Section:** Engaging "Discover Your Style" promotional banner with a direct "Shop Now" call to action[cite: 2].
* **Best Sellers Grid:** Showcases popular items such as a Premium Cotton T-Shirt, Premium Running Shoes, Classic Leather Jacket, and Elegant Women's Bag, complete with pricing and star ratings[cite: 2].
* **New Products Section:** Highlights the latest arrivals across various categories, including casual shirts, dresses, and travel backpacks[cite: 2].
* **Comprehensive Footer:** Contains brand information, quick navigation links, customer service options (My Account, Wishlist, Shopping Cart, Privacy Policy), and social media integration[cite: 2].
